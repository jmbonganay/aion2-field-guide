## Quick answer

The Sorcerer is one of the <Fact id="fact:global-class-count" label="eight" /> classes in AION 2 Global. Official sources available today confirm its place in the class list, its class-restricted weapon type, and a series of skill fixes since launch. They do **not** yet describe a build, rotation or stat priority, so this page doesn't recommend one.

## What official sources confirm

- **One of the Global classes.** The full list is <Fact id="fact:global-class-names" />.
- **Weapon type.** Class-restricted cosmetic weapon skins for the Sorcerer are of the <Fact id="fact:sorcerer-restricted-weapon-skin" /> type.
- **Named skills.** Three Sorcerer skills appear in Global patch notes: Fire Wall, Cold Storm and Bittercold Wind. Each is listed under Skills below with the fixes that changed it.

## Fixes since launch

Both weekly updates after launch corrected Sorcerer skills. Most fixes make the skills behave as described — for example, bonus damage now applies when you fight at a different height from your target, and another player's stronger Frostbite or Embers no longer removes yours. The Patch history section lists each update with its source.
