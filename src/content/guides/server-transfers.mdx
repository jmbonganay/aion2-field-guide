## Quick answer

A server transfer moves your characters to another server of the **same faction and region**. The current transfer period runs <Fact id="fact:st-period" />. You apply in <Fact id="fact:st-where" />, and you get **one transfer per account** for the whole period.

<Note tone="warning" title="Read this before you transfer">
You get one transfer per account for the whole period. Pets and Skins move with the characters you transfer, so any characters you leave behind lose them. Kina in Server Storage and items in Server Storage do **not** move.
</Note>

## Before you click Transfer

1. Collect Market sales and proceeds, Shop Storage items and mail on the character.
2. Move anything you need from Server Storage into the character's inventory or Character Storage.
3. Leave your Legion, finish or drop Duty missions, and stand in a village.
4. Check you want to move **all** characters that use your Pets and Skins.
5. Apply during your region's window in the in-game Shop.
