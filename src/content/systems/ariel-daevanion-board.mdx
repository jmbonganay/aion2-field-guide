## Quick answer

The Ariel Daevanion Board arrived with the <Term>Oct. 20 (PDT) / Oct. 21 (CEST)</Term> update. You earn its points by collecting Ariel Fragments from Expedition (Hard) and converting them through Substance Morph. Every fragment you convert into Enhance Stones is one you can't put towards the board.

## How Ariel Daevanion Points work

1. Clear [Krao Cave](/dungeons/krao-cave/) or [Draupnir](/dungeons/draupnir/) on Hard mode to collect Ariel Fragment (Bound).
2. Open <Fact id="fact:ariel-crystal-menu" />.
3. Convert <Fact id="fact:ariel-crystal-cost" label="100 Ariel Fragments" /> into one Daevanion Crystal: Ariel (Bound). You can do this up to <Fact id="fact:ariel-crystal-cap" label="30 times" /> per character.

The notes also say <Fact id="fact:ariel-per-dungeon-limit" />.

## The other use for fragments

Each Ariel Fragment can instead become <Fact id="fact:ariel-enhance-yield" label="5 Enhance Stones" />. This conversion has <Fact id="fact:ariel-enhance-cap" label="no limit" />, and you'll find it under <Fact id="fact:ariel-enhance-menu" />.

<Note tone="info" title="Editorial note — not a game mechanic">
Because both conversions use the same fragments, decide what you need first. The crystal conversion is capped per character and the Enhance Stone conversion isn't, so converting to Enhance Stones can wait. This is reasoning from the stated limits, not tested advice.
</Note>
