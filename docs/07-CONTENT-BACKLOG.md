## Expansion after launch
- Other Global classes (verify official list and names first), PvE/PvP builds, skills and stigmas.
- More dungeons and reward tables (all difficulties modeled separately).
- Weapon/armor/Guard/accessory database and acquisition paths.
- Pets spawn index and interactive map (only with legal/accurate map assets).
- Full Arcana card index, controlled comparators, validated formulas.
- Special events, patch-impact summaries, season-specific recommendations.
- Crafting and gathered-material database.

## Required fields in a guide research ticket
- Reader problem and answer promised.
- Applicable Global patch/version/difficulty.
- Source URLs and exact evidence found; confidence grade.
- Entity IDs involved; expected page links.
- Screenshots required and media-use clearance.
- Reviewer, status, last verified, target publish date.
- Risks and unresolved questions.

## Examples of disputed claims to RESEARCH, not assume
- Drop rates, guaranteed reward steps, currencies, pass item quantities.
- Exact calculations of GS gained by engravings or Daevanion points.
- Whether pet genus rolls accumulate globally or are preset-specific.
- Whether a 'best' class skill changed in a recent hotfix.
- Crafting materials / success percentages.

## Content quality minimum
- Short answer in first two paragraphs.
- Specific steps, limitations and alternative route where useful.
- 1+ trustworthy sources; high-risk points backed by first-party/current observation.
- Author and last verification date.
- Zero TODO strings, invented screenshots or promotional referral links.
