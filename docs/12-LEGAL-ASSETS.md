# 12 — Legal, Fan Content, Assets & Hosting

## Unofficial site disclaimer
Suggested footer/legal copy:
> This is an independent, unofficial AION 2 fan-made guide. AION 2 and related names, marks and artwork belong to their respective owners. This site is not endorsed by or affiliated with NCSOFT. In-game information may change with updates; please verify important details in the game and official announcements.

Do not use the game's official logo in a way that implies affiliation. Use a distinct identity. Do not misrepresent fansite status in domain, search snippets or social sharing.

## Game assets
- Before using icons, maps, official screenshots or characters, inspect NCSOFT's currently applicable fan content/press kit and IP usage terms (not assumed granted).
- If permitted, store specific rights/attribution facts in an asset manifest.
- Otherwise use original self-made illustrations, CSS/SVG icons and textual representations.
- Do not copy third-party wiki layouts, databases, interactive maps or article text in bulk.
- Attribution alone is not necessarily sufficient permission.
- Only use fonts under a compatible web license; never distribute commercial proprietary font files.

## Data acquisition
- Primary: manually curated and source-backed official patch notes plus lawful firsthand in-game testing.
- Secondary references can inform research; do not ingest whole proprietary databases without checking terms and licenses.
- Respect robots.txt, rate limits and terms; no scraping system is included in MVP.
- Do not embed 'live status' or game item data from unapproved third-party APIs.

## Free hosting restrictions
- Vercel Hobby: check eligibility as a **personal non-commercial** project and current limits before launch: https://vercel.com/docs/plans
- A `*.vercel.app` subdomain is included; custom domains may incur costs.
- Ads, sponsorships, referrals or commercial collaborations may require a different plan/host. Revisit policy before monetization.
- Traffic and free quotas are not unlimited; optimize pages and monitor usage.

## Privacy
No accounts, forms, cookies/analytics by default. Optional browser-local preferences do not transmit data. Add a short privacy statement reflecting actual implementation.

## Correction and liability
Provide a visible source trail and a way to report mistakes (optionally GitHub issue templates). State that gameplay outcomes/drops can vary and updates may invalidate older guidance.

## Attribution manifest example
```yaml
asset: /art/original/constellation.svg
creator: site team
source: original work
license: project-owned
permission_verified_at: 2026-10-22
use: website hero illustration
```
Never invent a license value for assets not reviewed.
